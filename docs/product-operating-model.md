# 11i Maps operating model

The product follows a measurable local-SEO loop: diagnose, plan, execute, measure and improve. Recommendations in the Synup proposal are treated as product inputs, not as proof that a provider capability is available.

## Available in the current release

- Tenant-isolated workspaces, roles, locations and one canonical NAP record.
- Evidence-based profile audits that create prioritised optimisation tasks.
- An Operations workspace showing real counts across the five-stage loop.
- AI review-reply drafts with low-rating replies held for explicit approval or dismissal.
- A tenant-scoped audit trail for location, audit, task and review decisions.
- Google Search Console OAuth, property selection and live 28-day search-performance reporting.
- Integration health that distinguishes connected, action-required and provider-gated states.

## Next after provider approval or a licensed data source

- Google Business Profile account/location import, profile updates, posts, media and review delivery.
- Bing Places and Apple Business Connect adapters where partner access permits writes.
- Scheduled listing-hygiene scans and multi-location publishing playbooks.
- Local rank heatmaps and competitor leaderboards using a licensed rank-grid provider.
- AI-search visibility measurement with reproducible prompts, cited sources and dated observations.
- Shareable, white-label client reports with scoped read-only access.

## Safety rules

- Never label a change as posted until the provider confirms it.
- Never fabricate rank, traffic, review or AI-visibility metrics.
- Hold sensitive replies for human approval; log the decision and provider outcome separately.
- Encrypt OAuth tokens, isolate every query by organization and retain least-privilege scopes.
- Show partial failures per location or provider instead of reporting a false all-platform success.
